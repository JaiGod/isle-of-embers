ISLE OF EMBERS

Stranded on a volcanic island. Gather, craft and build by day. Survive the raids by night. Light the signal beacon and escape.

▶ Play in browser

Free · Desktop (keyboard + mouse) & phone (touch) · Saves automatically · Made with Godot 4

What you'll do

🏝 Survive

Every island is procedurally generated. Keep Health, Hunger, Thirst and Warmth up through a full day / night cycle.

🪓 Gather & craft

Wood, stone, fiber and iron ore. 19 recipes across Hands, Workbench, Furnace and Campfire tiers: axe, pickaxe, spear, bow, iron sword, fire staff, armor.

🧱 Build a base

Campfires light and warm the night, walls stop raiders, chests store loot, farm plots grow berries, the furnace smelts iron.

🔥 Two bosses

The Thornback charges in on night 3. The Cinder Wyrm sleeps in the volcano crater, and its Ember Heart lights your way home.

Screenshots

Path to rescue

  1. Punch trees and boulders, then craft a Stone Axe and Pickaxe.
  2. Place a Workbench and a Campfire before the first night.
  3. Wall in your camp. Mine iron and smelt it at a Furnace.
  4. Beat the Thornback (night 3) and forge a Fire Staff from its shard.
  5. Wake the Cinder Wyrm in the crater and take its Ember Heart.
  6. Build the Signal Beacon, light it, and survive 60 seconds until the ship arrives.
